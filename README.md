### 📊 Research Works
**Seismic Performance of Elevated RCC Tanks with LRB Isolation Using Housner’s Model**

Primary Researcher|Under supervision of Dr. Kshitiz C. Shrestha |Presented at KtmGeoLab Student Research Symposium May 2026

- Evaluated performance of 100kL and 750kL DWSSM designed elevated RC water tanks using Housner's two-mass model in OpenSeesPy, comparing fixed-base and Lead Rubber Bearing (LRB) isolated systems.
- Performed pushover and NLTHA under NBC 105:2020 matched ground motions for Soil Type A.
- Demonstrated that LRB isolation reduced base shear by up to 30%, overturning moment by up to 65%, and increased drift capacity by up to 73%.
- Developed drift-based fragility curves, showing that base isolation enables smaller staging elements and shallower foundations, while requiring larger seismic gaps and flexible utility connections.

**Seismic Damage Assessment and Retrofit Strategies for RC Buildings with Rooftop Lattice Towers : A Kathmandu Valley Case Study**

Co-Researcher | Under supervision of Asst. Prof. Arun Poudel |Selected for ACI Proceedings of the 7th fib Congress 2026 Lisbon

- Investigated seismic vulnerability of five existing RC buildings with rooftop lattice towers in Kathmandu Valley, by performing NLTHA using code-defined local soil spectra. 
- Established new damage limits based on crack widths to enable practical field assessments.
- Developed fragility curves to assess the damage pattern at different probability of exceedance.
- Implemented a low-cost sisal based FRCM to restore its seismic performance.
- Developed a practical framework for rooftop tower installation, evaluation and policy support.

**Machine Learning-Based Framework for Real-Time Seismic Damage Assessment of RC Buildings at National Scale (Github)**

Primary Researcher|Under supervision of Dr. Kshitiz C. Shrestha | Submitted to Advances in Structural Engineering (ASE-26-0085)

- Conducted numerical simulations in OpenSeesPy, modeling 1900+ RC buildings across Nepal’s diverse construction practices (non-engineered, NBC 205:1994, NBC 205:2012, NBC 105:2020, NBC 205:2024).
- Performed NLTHA with local soil-specific spectra to evaluate seismic performance (first in this domain)
- Generated 54,000+ datasets and developed ML models for real-time seismic damage assessment.
- Identified key interacting features; mean, predominant, and natural vibration periods (via SHAP).
- Highlighted critical shortcomings in existing construction practices across local soil types.
- First large-scale, data-driven framework in Nepal, integrating advanced structural simulations with ML for identifying damage-susceptible designs and decision making.
